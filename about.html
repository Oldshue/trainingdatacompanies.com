About Training Data Companies

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Training Data Companies (trainingdatacompanies.com) is an independent research site. It answers one question: which companies supply AI training data, and which is the right fit for a given need.

What we are

  • We are an independent site that publishes research-based comparisons drawn from public sources: company websites, regulatory filings, company announcements and reputable press.
  • We are part of a small network of single-topic guide sites on AI tools and data.

What we are not

  • We have not tested these vendors' services hands-on, and we do not say we have.
  • We do not publish invented reviews, testimonials or ratings.
  • We do not sell rankings, and the companies listed did not pay to appear.
  • We do not claim staff credentials. The site is not run by named experts, and we do not attribute content to people who do not exist.

How to use this site

Use our ranking as a shortlist. Then ask vendors for samples or a pilot and judge them against your own requirements. Our methodology explains how the list was built and what its limits are.

Corrections

Companies change quickly. If a fact here is out of date, check the vendor's official site, which is linked from every entry. We revise pages when we confirm changes.
