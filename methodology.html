How we researched and ranked training data companies

Last updated: November 9, 2026

What we did

  1. Built a candidate list. We started with companies that 2026 market maps, funding datasets and industry reporting most often name as AI training data or human-data providers.
  2. Checked each company on its own website. For every company we read its official site, as it appeared on November 9, 2026, to confirm what it currently sells: expert data, RL environments, evaluations, annotation, off-the-shelf datasets and platforms. TELUS Digital's site blocked our automated reader. For TELUS Digital we used its pages as summarized by our research search, plus TELUS's own press release.
  3. Added current reporting. For ownership, funding and leadership we used primary filings (Meta's 2025 Form 10-K), company announcements and reputable press (Forbes, TIME, CNBC).
  4. Recorded pricing only where it is published. Prolific is the only company on the list with a public fee schedule. For the rest we describe the pricing model and do not guess at prices.

Ranking criteria

  • Fit for modern needs: post-training data, RL environments, evaluations and agent data, as well as classic annotation.
  • Depth of expertise and workforce: verified domain experts, quality systems and published QA practices.
  • Scale and momentum: reported revenue, run rate, customers and 2026 developments, treated as rough signals.
  • Breadth of modalities: text, code, vision, audio, sensor, robotics.
  • Transparency: public detail about offerings, licensing, security and pricing.
  • Buyer risks: ownership conflicts, leadership changes and integration after acquisitions.

Important caveats

  • We have not tested these services hands-on and we publish no user reviews. Descriptions reflect public information.
  • Private-company figures mix audited revenue, gross run rate, self-reported numbers and valuations, so they are not directly comparable. We label them as reported or self-reported, and secondary compilations are named as such.
  • Rankings are editorial judgments for a general buyer. The right vendor for your project depends on your data type, domain and budget.
  • No company paid to be included or to be ranked higher.

Updates

We review the list when material events happen, such as funding, acquisitions, leadership changes or pricing changes, and show the date of the latest review at the top of each page.
